v0.1.58: multi-wallbox support. Sites with more than one wallbox now get one HA sub-device per physical wallbox, vehicles parent under the wallbox they are actually paired to, and a per-wallbox connectivity sensor lets automations react to a single wallbox going offline.

## Added

**Per-wallbox sub-devices.** If your site has more than one wallbox, each physical wallbox now appears as its own sub-device under the system parent with its own manufacturer, model and firmware — instead of collapsing to a single "Wallbox" device. Single-wallbox installs (the overwhelming majority) are unchanged.

**Vehicle profiles parent under their paired wallbox.** With more than one wallbox in place, each vehicle profile (`EVCharger`) via_devices onto the wallbox it is actually paired to (via `Wallbox.assigned_ev_id ↔ EVCharger.assigned_charger_id`). HA's device view now renders "Vehicle → Wallbox → System" for the natural relationship instead of parking every vehicle under the same wallbox device.

**Per-wallbox connectivity sensors.** New `binary_sensor.<sys>_wallbox_<id>_connected` per physical wallbox on multi-wallbox setups, so automations can gate on one specific wallbox (`if wallbox_garage_connected == off then notify …`). The aggregate `wallbox_connected` (AND over all wallboxes) stays as the default "all wallboxes healthy" gate. Single-wallbox setups do not get the per-instance sensor; the aggregate already carries the same information.

## Changed

**Duplicate-asset warning gone.** The system-status coordinator no longer logs `Duplicate asset of type EV_CHARGER; keeping first, dropping subsequent` when it sees more than one wallbox. Multiple wallboxes are legitimate and the coordinator surfaces the full list.

## Compat

The single-wallbox case (the historical default) keeps the wallbox sub-device identifier `(onekommafive, <system_id>_wallbox)` and the translated `device.wallbox.name` label. Area assignments, dashboards and every existing device_registry reference stay intact — no migration, no renamed entities. Unique IDs and entity IDs of vehicle-related entities are unchanged for both single- and multi-wallbox installs, so long-term statistics, automations and Energy-Dashboard configuration keep working across the update.
