v0.1.56 — hotfix for the FoxESS battery sub-device. No behavioural changes elsewhere.

## Fixed

**Battery entities now sit on the battery sub-device on FoxESS installs.** v0.1.54 introduced a dedicated `battery` sub-device for installs where the 1KOMMA5° cloud reports a separate `BATTERY` asset (FoxESS and similar), but the four battery-related entities were still hard-wired to the inverter sub-device — so the new device stayed empty and HA didn't create it. This release re-routes them:

- `sensor.<sys>_battery_power`
- `sensor.<sys>_battery_soc`
- `sensor.<sys>_battery_charge_power_energy`
- `sensor.<sys>_battery_discharge_power_energy`

now materialise under a proper **Batterie** / **Battery** sub-device on FoxESS installs.

Sungrow (and any single-`HYBRID` install) are unchanged — no empty battery device is grown when the cloud reports no separate `BATTERY` asset. Unique-ids and entity-ids stay stable across releases; you may want to re-check area assignments if you'd manually placed the four battery sensors' inverter device into a specific area.
