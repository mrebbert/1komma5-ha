v0.1.61: adds the `BATTERY_DISCHARGE_TO_GRID` optimization decision, translated in all seven locales.

## Fixed

**Optimization decision `BATTERY_DISCHARGE_TO_GRID`.** The 1KOMMA5° Cloud started shipping this decision on 2026-09-22 when the battery discharges into the grid at high spot prices (first observed on a Swedish site with SEK-denominated market prices, reported in issue #23). Before this patch the value coerced to `unknown` and the log carried a one-line warning; the sensor stays functional but the decision was not surfaced. `sensor.<sys>_optimization_last_decision` now lists the value in its ENUM `options` and translates it in every locale (German: "Batterie ins Netz entladen", English: "Discharge battery to grid", and analogously for da/es/fi/nl/sv). Regression test pins the mapping.
