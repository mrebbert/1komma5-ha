v0.1.48 — no schema changes, no migrations. Purely additive: four new read-only fields plus price-breakdown attributes, all surfaced from an SDK-surface audit and verified against live data.

## New

**`sensor.<sys>_daily_savings`** — today's cloud-computed energy savings (€), read directly from 1KOMMA5° (`get_energy_today`). A daily running total that resets at local midnight (`state_class=total`, `last_reset` at the start of the local day), so it feeds Long-Term Statistics as one value per day. Backed by a new energy coordinator (15-min refresh) with its own `diag_energy_update` diagnostic timestamp. Currency follows your account.

**Two EV vehicle sensors** surfacing fields already present in the EV profile:
- `sensor.<sys>_ev_battery_capacity` — the vehicle's nominal battery capacity (kWh).
- `sensor.<sys>_ev_scheduled_departure_soc` — the departure schedule's target SoC (%), distinct from the manual *Target SoC*. Read-only.

**Grid-cost breakdown on `sensor.<sys>_current_electricity_price`** — new attributes answering "why is my kWh this price?":
- `spot_price` — raw market price of the active slot (net).
- `grid_costs` — net grid-cost adder, with `grid_cost_components` breaking it out (energy tax, purchasing, fixed tariff, dynamic markup, feed-in adjustment).
- `vat_rate` and `uses_fallback_grid_costs` (a data-quality flag).

The price decomposes exactly as `all_in = (spot_price + grid_costs) × (1 + vat_rate)`. The components are net (ex-VAT) daily constants; only `spot_price` varies per 15-minute slot.

## Upgrade

Nothing required. All additions are new entities/attributes — no `entity_id` / `unique_id` changes, no statistics discontinuity, no config migration. Existing dashboards, automations and Energy-Dashboard config are unaffected.

The new EV and daily-savings entities are enabled by default; disable any you don't need from the entity registry.
