v0.1.51 — maintenance release. Internal refactor sweep plus a low-risk SDK bump. One service-scope fix for `refresh_now`. No new entities, no schema changes, no migrations.

## Fixed

**`onekommafive.refresh_now` finally covers the energy coordinator.** The service accepts `coordinator: energy` and `coordinator: all` now includes the energy coordinator that backs `sensor.<sys>_daily_savings`. Before this fix the energy coordinator (shipped in v0.1.48) was silently missing from the selector, schema and handler — automations that forced a refresh via `all` were touching five of the six coordinators. UI selector, schema and the DE description ("alle fünf" → "alle sechs") are now in sync.

## Changed

**SDK pin raised to `>=0.1.40,<0.2`.** The upstream `onekommafive` SDK added a large batch of new read-only endpoints across `0.1.24` – `0.1.40` (Heartbeat savings, CO2/impact overview, energy-trader status, AI summary, notifications, and more) alongside internal robustness fixes. No breaking changes to endpoints this integration consumes; every existing entity keeps its `unique_id`, `entity_id` and translation key. The new endpoints are not surfaced as Home Assistant entities in this release — follow-up releases will pick from them.

**Internal refactor sweep across `entity.py`, `binary_sensor.py` and `sensor_entities.py`.**
- `apply_stable_entity_ids` now takes the platform-domain string directly. Call sites collapse from `f"{DOMAIN}.{{}}"` to `DOMAIN`.
- `OneKomma5DiagnosticSensor` folded onto the generic `_BaseSystemEntity` — one MRO, no duplicated `CoordinatorEntity` boilerplate.
- Four near-clone binary sensors (Optimization Battery-grid-charge / Heat-pump-recommended, EnergyTrader-active / DynamicPulse-compatible) collapse into two spec-driven classes (`OneKomma5OptimizationDecisionSensor`, `OneKomma5DetailsFlagSensor`).
- Shared plumbing of the today / tomorrow cheapest-window sensors extracted into `_ChargingWindowBase`.
- Trimmed multi-paragraph AI-noise comments; `_DEVICE_KEY_BY_ASSET_TYPE` eliminated.

No `unique_id` changes, no translation-key changes, no `entity_id` changes. Existing entities keep their state, statistics and dashboards untouched. Net −66 lines across the two files.

## Upgrade

Nothing required. Purely internal refactor plus a dependency constraint. `entity_id`s and long-term statistics are unaffected.
