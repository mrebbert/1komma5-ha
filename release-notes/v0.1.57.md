v0.1.57 — wallbox / EV control now works on non-GridX 1KOMMA5° installs. Plus cleaner diagnostics and Repair-issue behaviour on newer backends.

## Added

**Charging-mode / target-SoC / departure-time entities on non-GridX installs.** If your setup uses a non-GridX wallbox (e.g. Enphase EVSE_IQ2, or any newer 1K5-backend install), these entities were missing until now because the SDK's wallbox/EV endpoints returned `error_code:30401 "DeviceGateway not found"` on the legacy system-scoped routes. The `onekommafive` SDK is raised to `>=0.2.0,<0.3` in this release; upstream v0.2.0 moved these endpoints to the site-scoped v2 routes that work on both GridX and non-GridX backends.

To pick the entities up after updating:

1. Make sure a vehicle profile is registered under *Settings → Vehicles* in the 1KOMMA5° app and paired to the wallbox.
2. Restart Home Assistant.
3. `select.<sys>_ev_charging_mode`, `number.<sys>_ev_target_soc`, `time.<sys>_ev_departure_time`, and the related sensors should show up on your dashboard.

If they still don't, download diagnostics and check `data.system.wallboxes[].assigned_ev_id_present` — it's `false` when no vehicle is paired.

**Notifications coordinator in the diagnostics dump.** The notifications coordinator was silently missing from the `coordinators` section of the diagnostics download; it's now included with a small `count` summary alongside the other six coordinators.

## Changed

**EMS auto-mode switch is no longer created on 1K5-backend installs.** The underlying GridX EMS endpoint is unreachable on `emp_type: "1K5"` setups, so the switch would be permanently unavailable and the Repair Issue `ems_settings_unavailable` would fire on every setup after a few refresh cycles. Both are now skipped when the backend can't serve them. GridX installs are unchanged.

**Wallbox diagnostics block: `hardware_id_present` replaces `gridx_hardware_id_present`.** SDK v0.2.0 renamed `Wallbox.gridx_hardware_id` to a generic `Wallbox.id` — the diagnostics field now reflects that. The `emp_type_1k5_native_hint` triage flag stays as a safety net for edge cases where even the new site-scoped route fails.
